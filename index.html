Built for SOC analysts, SOC managers, red teamers, CISOs, and MSSP operators.

Competent security work is gated behind expertise most teams can't hire.

Three roles carry front-line security work. Each one hits a different wall.

Red teamers

Penetration testing needs scarce experts. Without a modular way to chain tools into a reusable sequence, assessments happen rarely and unevenly.

Blue teamers

Investigating an incident means reading large volumes of logs by hand. Separating real threats from noise takes more time than an active incident allows.

L1 analysts

They decide what to escalate, often without the training to understand the attack. Searching the web mid-incident slows the response and can make it worse.

Violet lowers that gate without lowering the quality of the work.

One workspace for both sides of the house.

Offensive testing, log analysis, automated response, and a shared knowledge base, wired together so findings on one side inform the other.

Red team

Every assessment starts with proof of ownership.

Violet runs a scan only against targets your tenant has verified it owns. The platform enforces this rule in SaaS and on-prem, so a scan outside scope is refused, not just discouraged.

  1. Verify ownership

    Prove each target with a DNS TXT record, a verification file, a verified cloud account, or a signed authorization from a tenant admin for internal ranges.

  2. Set the rules of engagement

    Declare the allowed ranges, node types, time window, and an intensity ceiling. Adding a target needs admin approval.

  3. Build and validate

    Violet checks the flow before it runs and names the problem: an authentication failure, an incompatible configuration, a duplicate connection, or an unsupported node type.

  4. Run sandboxed

    Each node runs isolated and resource-limited. Validation checks default to safe mode, and destructive actions stay off unless the engagement explicitly allows them.

  5. Read the report

    The AI report summarizes the attack surface, the vulnerabilities found, and the mitigations to apply, with a risk score per finding.

Blue team

From raw logs to a contained incident in seconds.

Violet reads the logs, ranks what matters, and runs your response flow the moment a matching incident appears. Common incidents are handled before anyone opens a ticket.

  1. Ingest

    Connect a SIEM, IDS/IPS, EDR, cloud platform, or IoT source for continuous ingestion, or upload JSON, CSV, or XML. Ingestion applies back-pressure under load instead of dropping events.

  2. Summarize and score

    AI turns large log volumes into a readable report: highlighted anomalies, identified risks, and recommended mitigations, with a risk score for every event.

  3. Respond automatically

    A matching incident starts its response flow in seconds. Connected firewalls, EDR, and identity systems carry out the actions.

  4. Keep a human in the loop

    High-impact actions wait for approval. Every automated action is logged and reversible where feasible, and a failed action raises an alert instead of passing silently.

Your model, your keys, your data.

Every AI feature goes through one layer, so the model provider is a setting, not a dependency. AI summarizes logs, scores risk, writes reports, and answers questions. It does not act on its own.

Anthropic Google Gemini OpenAI Self-hosted open model

Choose per tenant

Pick a default model and override it per feature, such as a lower-cost model for summaries and a stronger one for reports.

Bring your own key

Keys are encrypted per tenant and never logged. Usage bills to your own provider account under your own agreement.

Run fully offline

Air-gapped installs use a self-hosted open model, so no prompt leaves your network.

Advisory by design

AI never runs an offensive action or a response flow without the approval and scope checks. Prompts and outputs stay with their tenant.

Everything outside Violet is a connector.

Each integration and tool is a signed, versioned package. Install it from the catalog, configure it, and it appears as nodes, log sources, and actions. It runs sandboxed, with only the network access and secrets its manifest declares.

Built to the standard of the systems it protects.

Violet holds your raw security data and runs offensive tooling. The controls that keep it safe are first-class features, not settings.

Tenant isolation in the database

Every row is scoped to its tenant and enforced below the application. Automated tests check isolation on every change.

Ownership before any scan

The platform refuses a scan against any target your tenant hasn't verified, in SaaS and on-prem alike.

Sandboxed connectors

Each connector runs isolated, reaches only the hosts its manifest declares, and receives secrets only for the duration of a call.

Signed connectors and releases

Everything is signed and verified before it runs, including offline bundles on the appliance.

Secrets in a vault

Credentials and API keys are encrypted per tenant and never written to logs or the configuration database.

Immutable audit log

Every scan, published flow, connector install, and role change is recorded with the actor, the time, and the state before and after.

Roles and separation of duties

Owner, Admin, Flow Author, Analyst, and Read-only roles. High-risk actions need an elevated role and can require a separate approver.

Encryption and supply chain

Encryption in transit and at rest, pinned and scanned dependencies, and a software bill of materials for the appliance image.
