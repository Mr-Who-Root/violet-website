The reality for security teams

Good security work needs experts. Most teams can't hire enough of them.

Red teamers chain tools by hand, so assessments happen rarely. Blue teamers read logs line by line while an incident unfolds. L1 analysts decide what to escalate without the depth to understand the attack.

None of them is doing it wrong. The expertise is gated. Violet lowers that gate without lowering the quality of the work.

One workspace

Test your defenses. Read your logs. Respond in seconds.

Red team canvas

Drag scan nodes onto a canvas, validate the flow, and run it against targets you have verified. AI writes the report and scores every finding.

Blue team console

Ingest logs from your SIEM, EDR, and IDS/IPS. AI summarizes what matters and scores every event, so analysts start at the top of the list.

Automated response

Build flows from triggers, conditions, actions, and API calls. Common incidents are contained in seconds, and risky actions wait for approval.

Knowledge base

Ask how to stop a privilege-escalation attempt and get a grounded answer in context, instead of searching the web mid-incident.

Violet runs as SaaS or as an appliance on your own hardware, including fully air-gapped sites.

Why Violet?

Security operations without the headcount.

Violet is built for the team you have: it does the expert work in the background and keeps a person in charge of every action that matters.

Ownership before every scan. The platform refuses any target your tenant hasn't verified, in SaaS and on-prem.

Contained in seconds. Response flows act the moment a matching incident appears, and every action lands in the audit log.

Your model, your keys. Choose Anthropic, Google, OpenAI, or a local model per tenant. AI advises; people approve.

Isolation you can test. Each tenant's data is separated in the database, and automated tests check it on every change.

Deployment

In our cloud or on your own hardware.

One codebase ships three ways, with the same features in each. Licenses validate offline, so a lapsed license never blinds your SOC.

SaaS. We host Violet in the region you choose. Use our managed AI keys or bring your own.

On-prem appliance. An ISO or VM image activated by a license key, updated with signed packages you apply.

Air-gapped. No outbound connection. A local AI model and signed offline bundles for connectors and updates.
